# Red day review, 2026-11-19: every book lost

Owner, the night of 2026-11-19: "results are better but still 100% loss, which is very obviously bad. same for all of
these books that are at a loss, not a single one green". The nightly summary: longs -$351 (3 trades, 0 won), megacap
long calls -$2,539 (-2.54%), megacap -1.83%, megacap 15 -2.58%, overlay -2.77%, 60/40 -1.11%, fades no trade.

The statement is true. This review says why each book was red, what the tests behind each book say about a day like
this, and what the review found wrong on our side. Everything read from stored records is description of data
already seen; nothing here is a finding that changes a rule.

## 1. What happened

All 21 large caps of the daily model fell from the close of 11-18 to the close of 11-19: average -2.80%, none up.
It was the fifth falling session in a row for their average: -0.7%, -2.5%, -1.0%, -0.9%, -2.8%, about -8% together.

| Book | Result | What it held | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| megacap long calls | -2.54% | 9 long calls (AMZN, MSFT, NVDA, RDDT, JPM, MA, ORCL, V, AAOI), about 85% invested | the 9 calls made -2.95% on average; all 21 names made -2.80%. The calls were the market, no better and no worse |
| megacap (6 names) | -1.83% | long AMZN, MSFT, NVDA; no short call | same |
| megacap 15 | -2.58% | long RDDT, JPM, MA, ORCL, V, AAOI | same |
| overlay, 60/40, dual momentum | -1.1% to -2.8% | baskets held all day | the market |
| alert longs | -$351, 3 of 3 lost | POAS, GP, MRKR on the one-minute entry | `long_bot_replay_2026-11-19.md`: this side has no tested edge; the entry was replaced the same evening |
| alert fades | no trade | nothing | every alert was stopped by a gate (section 3) |

Every book that held anything held it long, and the day was down. No book in the system makes money from a falling
market: the model's short calls have no tested edge (+0.01% a call, n 1,448, section 6b of the playbook), and the
fade shorts small-cap spikes, which has nothing to do with the market's direction.

## 2. The daily model: is a day like this inside its tested record?

Read from the model's own out-of-sample record, 2018 to 2026, 21 names, 4,724 long calls
(`reports/megacap_daily_v2`, `reports/megacap_oos`; stored predictions, nothing refitted).

**What its long calls are.** A long call made +0.32% the next day before costs (+0.28% after); the average of the 21
names on the same days made +0.29%, so a call beat the average name by +0.03% and did so 48% of the time. The playbook already says this (section 6b: "a
small, fading dip-buying tilt on large caps; not a stock picker"). The calls earn what the market earns the day
after it falls:

| State at the decision | Long calls | Next day, after costs | Wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| the 21 names up on the day | 1,956 | -0.01% | 52% |
| down 0 to 1% | 1,270 | +0.24% | 52% |
| down 1 to 2% | 685 | +0.36% | 55% |
| down 2% or more | 813 | +0.98% | 59% |
| five-day move above -2% | 2,830 | +0.01% | 51% |
| five-day move -2% to -5% | 961 | +0.27% | 56% |
| five-day move below -5% | 933 | +1.12% | 61% |

So the model buys hardest exactly when the market has been falling, and that is where its whole tested average
comes from. When the fall goes on another day, those are its worst days. 11-19 was one.

**How bad, against the record.** Days with 9 or more long calls: 73 in the record, average +0.91%, 59% of them up.
Every call lost on 6 of the 73. The day's average was below -2.5% on 3 of the 73. 11-19 (-2.95% on 9 calls) is in
that worst twentieth. A known tail of the bet, not a break in the code.

**The forward record so far is poor and too short to judge.** Since 2026-11-16: 12 long calls, 2 won, average
-2.24%; the deciding equal-weight ledger -1.28% while holding the 21 names made -4.66%. A three-session fall of the
ledger that large happened in 1.4% of the three-session windows of the record. 12 calls on three days are three
observations. The registered stop (-5% on the ledger, enforced in code) stands; the worst drawdown of the ledger in
the whole record was -5.0%.

**A market hedge is not a fix.** Hedged with the average of the 21 names, a long call keeps +0.03% before costs. The
hedge would make the book calm on a day like 11-19 by removing the thing it earns.

**Found on our side: the paper book swings about twice the ledger that decides it.** The ledger gives every call
1/21 of the book (9 calls = 43% invested). The paper book sizes under `MEGACAP_SIZING` (up to 25% a stock, gross
1.0): 9 calls were about 85% invested. Hence -2.54% in the book against -1.28% in the ledger on the same calls. The
tested numbers of section 6b (Sharpe 0.92, worst drawdown -6%) are the ledger's. The stop reads the ledger, so the
book can be down about twice the stop's -5% when it fires. This is how the test was registered
(`prereg_megacap_long_forward_2026-11-13`) and how the owner set the size (2026-11-14); it is stated here so the
dollar figure in the summary is read correctly. Not changed.

**State for 2026-11-20.** The model decided 14 long calls at 15:50 on 11-19 (about 97% of the paper book). The
five-day move of the 21 names is below -5%: the row of the table with the best tested average and, by the same
logic, the largest loss if the selling continues. The down-day second ledger starts with this session.
