# Learning pass — 2026-10-17

**Data:** 207–304 symbols discovered by yfinance screeners, 19 sessions (2026-09-16 … 2026-10-12) of 1-minute bars, plus 3 unseen sessions (2026-10-13 … 10-15). Cost model: half-spread 1.0% (<$1), 0.5% ($1–5), 0.2% ($5–20), 0.05% (>$20) per side, ×1.5 for float < 5M. These are assumptions, not measured quotes; the conclusions depend on them.

## 1. Why trader_v0 loses

| Run | Trades | Before costs | After costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| trader_v0 | 21–33 | −0.12% / trade | −0.57% / trade |
| Every 9/24 cross (wide universe) | ~18–32K | **+0.03% / trade** | −0.21% / trade |

Across 39,428 crossover signals, the average move 15 minutes after a cross is **−0.01% before costs**, against a modeled **0.38% round-trip cost**. The crossover carries no directional information on this universe; costs make it a steady loss. Every time-of-day window, power hour included, is ≈ 0 before costs.

## 2. Where a move does exist

- Low-float stocks (5–20M shares): +0.40% gross at 15 minutes (n = 1,134).
- Low float **and** a volume spike (bar relative volume > 1): +0.11% to +0.24% **net** at 30 minutes (n = 210).
- Large volume spikes (> 3×) move +1.5% in 15 minutes and give back half by 30 minutes (pump-and-fade).

## 3. Why that isn't a strategy yet

- Unstable: the low-float + volume pocket lost in the first half of the period (−0.93% net) and made money in the second (+1.31%).
- Concentrated: 9 symbols; GENI and ACMR are over half the signals.
- No profit-target / stop / time-limit combination (64 tested) was positive on the held-out half.
- trader_v1, built from these findings: −$518 in-sample (112 trades; the −2% stop hit 60 times) and −$12 on the 3 unseen sessions (26 trades, −0.22% / trade).

## 4. Learned filter

A logistic / LightGBM filter trained on earlier sessions and scored on later ones separates winners from losers (AUC ≈ 0.68 in all 3 folds). Its top-decile signals still average −0.01% to −0.21% net: it reduces losses, mostly by avoiding expensive-to-trade names, but does not create an edge.

## 5. Decisions

- **No live trading.** No strategy passes the held-out test; the capital ladder already blocks live trading without a validated baseline.
- trader_v1 is a **shadow-only hypothesis**.
- Next evidence needed: (a) the trader's actual alerted tickers (Telegram), since screener-discovered names are mostly large-float; (b) real quotes (Alpaca SIP) to replace assumed spreads, which dominate the result; (c) more sessions.
- Fixed during this pass: backtests could trade beyond account size (F-15, buying-power cap); a quadratic slowdown in order scanning (PR #3).
